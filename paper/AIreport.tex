\textbf{1)DeepSeek (December 1,2025,V3.2)}

\textbf{Query: }

What websites can I find the failure rate of rocket launching missions of various countries or organizations in recent years?

\textbf{Output:}

At present, there is no single website to fully summarize the global launch failure rate, but it can be obtained through the following ways:

\textbf{a.}	Official website of space agency

NASA, ESA, CNSA, SpaceX and other companies will issue mission reports, some of which include reliability statistics.

\textbf{b.}	Professional database

Gunter's Space Page (space.skyrocket.de): detailed task list, including the failure mark.
Jonathan's Space Report: independently records the success or failure of the launch.
Union of Concerned Scientists (ucsusa.org) satellite database: it can filter the launch status.

\textbf{c.}	Annual report

SpaceLaunch Report (spacelaunchreport.com) counts success and failure by year.
Bryce Tech (formerly Euroconsult) released the annual launch market report with reliability data.

\textbf{d.}	Media and wiki

SpaceNews, NASASpaceFlight tracking report.
The annual page of Wikipedia "List of spaceflight launches" has a success or failure classification, but it needs to be cross checked.

It is recommended to combine multi-source data comparative analysis, because the statistical caliber (such as partial failure, launch stage definition) may be different.
