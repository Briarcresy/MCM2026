\textbf{Query:}

I need you to provide me with some clues and suggestions. What kind of mathematical distribution should I know and use to more scientifically simulate the failure frequency and failure time of rockets and space elevators?

\textbf{Output:}

For modeling failure frequency, Poisson distribution is commonly used to describe the number of random failures in a fixed task cycle (such as a single launch).

For the time between failures,the exponential distribution is suitable for the random failure stage with constant failure rate.

Weibull distribution is the most critical and flexible. Its shape parameters can describe the complete life cycle of decreasing failure rate (early failure), constant (random failure) or increasing (wear aging). This is essential for the modeling of space elevator components with fatigue and wear.

The core proposal: the rocket short-term mission can focus on Poisson and exponential distribution; The long-term cycle operation and structural aging of space elevator must be modeled with Weibull distribution. The final selection needs to be determined by fitting historical or simulation data.
